Displate

Every screen, one slider.

Brightness, contrast and resolution for every display you plug in, including the monitors your keyboard has never been able to dim.

  • F1 and F2 work on external monitors. Over DDC, the same wire your monitor's own buttons use.
  • Five resolutions, not forty. From larger text to more space, with the default marked.
  • Sharp text on any monitor. Switch to HiDPI with one key, or unlock it where macOS hides it.
Asks for Accessibility access once, for the brightness keys.
Unload

Get your memory back.

See what is really holding your RAM and free it in one click. Made for anyone running local models who is tired of restarting to get 8 GB back.

  • Finds the real culprit. Counts model weights llama.cpp and Ollama keep in memory, which Activity Monitor leaves out.
  • Unloads models without quitting. One button per model loaded in Ollama or LM Studio.
  • Quit anything, safely. Force Quit when an app hangs. WindowServer and friends are protected.
No permissions needed.
Breeze

Quiet until it matters.

Temperatures at a glance, and fans that follow a curve you draw. Silent while you write, full speed only when a render needs it.

  • Draw your own fan curve. Or start from Quiet, Balanced or Performance.
  • Follows the sensor you choose. CPU, GPU, or whichever runs hottest.
  • Always hands control back. Fans return to automatic whenever Breeze quits.
Installs a small helper once, with your password, to set fan speed.
